# SilkierStrands Site Profile

SilkierStrands is a consumer editorial site for women seeking straightforward, evidence-aware hair-care product reviews and styling-tool comparisons. The brand voice is polished, warm, practical, and candid. It should feel like a knowledgeable beauty editor explaining trade-offs to a reader, not a sales page, medical provider, social post, or laboratory report. Favor specific routine guidance, texture and hair-type fit, usability, and value framing over hype.

## Audience and approved categories

Primary readers are adults comparing hair-care products or tools for fine, thick, dry, coarse, curly, normal, color-treated, frizzy, wavy, damaged, oily, blonde, gray, coily, or thinning-looking hair. Approved product categories are exactly: `Shampoo & Conditioner` (`shampoo-conditioner`), `Hair Masks & Treatments` (`hair-masks`), `Serums & Oils` (`serums-oils`), `Hair Dryers` (`hair-dryers`), `Flat Irons & Straighteners` (`flat-irons`), and `Curling Irons & Wands` (`curling-irons`). Do not create categories, topics, or hair-type labels outside the provided index and this profile unless they are already demonstrably supported by the site data.

## Voice and editorial limits

Use concise paragraphs and explain who should choose a product, why, and what trade-off matters. Avoid first-person testing claims because the structured content source does not carry substantiated test protocols. Use qualified language such as “may suit,” “is designed for,” or “is a better fit” where the evidence is product positioning or editorial context. Do not assert that a consumer outcome is guaranteed. Do not describe a product as medical treatment; do not advise on hair-loss diagnosis, scalp disease, pregnancy, medication, or any other medical topic. Do not make before-and-after, regrowth, healing, cure, or clinical-result claims without direct, appropriately qualified evidence.

## Commerce rules

Affiliate links are rendered by the application only. Generated content must never include raw Amazon URLs, CTA text, marketplace badges, pricing promises, or disclosure markup. Retain citations in the structured record. The site-wide layout provides the Amazon Associate disclosure. Exact ASIN-to-product mapping and image availability are verified separately before any shadow branch is pushed.

## Content shape

Prefer one to three tightly related product reviews and, where appropriate, one or more comparisons that use published IDs or the newly generated IDs. Do not pad a weekly batch to a predetermined item count. A review is valuable only when its product identity, useful evidence, target user, and limitations are clear. A comparison is valuable only when it helps a reader choose between two legitimate alternatives.
